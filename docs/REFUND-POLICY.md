# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Product:** Doctoral Second Brain  
**Last updated:** October 14, 2026  
**Billing:** Stripe Customer Portal from your account dashboard  
**Support:** rich.huebner@nantasket.ai

## Canceling

You may cancel at any time in the Stripe Customer Portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period unless we agree otherwise in writing.

After the paid period ends (and any 3-day `past_due` grace):

- the `doctoral_brain_full` entitlement becomes inactive;
- GitHub team / collaborator access is revoked;
- marketplace updates and new installs stop;
- local copies must not be shared or redistributed.

You keep access through the end of the period you already paid for.

## Refunds

Subscriptions are generally non-refundable once the paid period has started, because repository access is granted immediately after Stripe confirms payment.

We will consider a full refund of the most recent charge if **all** of the following are true:

1. you request it within **7 days** of the initial purchase (not a renewal); and
2. we can confirm that GitHub access was never accepted, or you ask us to revoke it as part of the refund.

Renewals, unused time after the first week, and change-of-mind requests after you have cloned or installed the plugin are not refundable.

If we issue a refund, access is revoked when Stripe reports the refund, and the license for redistribution does not resume.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Stripe retries according to its dunning settings. Entitlement stays active for **3 days** in `past_due` status, then we revoke GitHub access. Updating your payment method in the Customer Portal and paying the open invoice restores access after Stripe confirms `active`.

## Chargebacks

A dispute or chargeback is treated as a request to end the subscription. We revoke access when we receive the dispute event and may decline to reinstate the same account without contacting support.

## How to request a refund

Email rich.huebner@nantasket.ai from the billing email on the subscription. Include the Stripe receipt email or the last four digits of the card and the approximate purchase date. Do not send full card numbers.

We aim to respond within two business days.
